02 / A little about me

Curiosity first.
Design always.

I’m Ravishka Nimsara, a graphic and creative designer from Sri Lanka who enjoys turning ideas into strong visual stories. I work across branding, posters, social media campaigns, promotional materials and digital artwork.

I love experimenting with typography, composition, colour and photo manipulation — balancing creative expression with clear communication. Whether it’s a brand identity, an event graphic or a promotional design, my goal is to make every piece feel intentional, memorable and made for its audience.
